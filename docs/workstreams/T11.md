# T11: Reliability, Monitoring & Operations

Owner expertise: SRE. No individual assignment implied.

## Research question

Define availability, latency, recovery and capacity objectives from expected guild/board use with measured baseline plan.

## Required contract

Specify metrics/log privacy, alert thresholds, backup/restore acceptance and incident responsibility without invented SLA.

## Capability scope

### Observability

- **Structured logs and error reporting**: Emit request/operation/board correlation and classified errors; redact tokens and content; define retention, access and useful error reporting without sensitive payloads.

- **Connection, board and resource metrics**: Measure active sockets/boards, operation latency, queue depth, CPU/RAM, database health and disk; bound metric cardinality and configure actionable alerts from measured budgets.

### Service resilience

- **Graceful shutdown and restart behavior**: Stop new work, drain accepted operations, close sockets with reconnect guidance and flush telemetry; verify systemd/Compose restart preserves state and avoids duplicate acknowledgments.

- **Performance and capacity planning**: Load test realistic boards, assets and concurrent editors; identify CPU/database/disk bottlenecks and documented scale limits; define triggers for VPS resizing or topology change.

### Backup and disaster recovery

- **Database and asset backups with retention**: Create consistent encrypted database/asset backups, retention schedule and integrity checks; restrict backup credentials and storage access; monitor missing/failed backups.

- **Restore testing and disaster recovery acceptance**: Restore isolated fresh environment from backup plus required logs/assets; measure achieved RPO/RTO and reconcile board hashes; prove credential recovery and document data-loss boundaries.

### Operational readiness

- **Runbooks, maintenance and incident response**: Document deploy/rollback, patching, disk pressure, outage triage, on-call ownership and communications; rehearse operational scenarios before production acceptance.

- **Security incident handling and post-release care**: Define credential revocation, isolation, forensic preservation and disclosure routing; maintain dependency updates, backup checks and post-incident improvement backlog.
