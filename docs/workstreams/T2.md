# T2: Authorization & Moderation

Owner expertise: Security & Authorization. No individual assignment implied.

## Research question

Resolve role mapping precedence, required Discord privileges and revocation freshness guarantees with explicit abuse cases.

## Required contract

Define deny-by-default permission matrix for viewer, writer, moderator and administrator, tenant scoping and immediate revocation semantics.

## Capability scope

### Permission policy

- **Role mapping and administrator onboarding**: Configure Discord role mappings through verified guild administrators; define viewer/writer/moderator/admin precedence and explicit-deny rules; prevent users from editing their own privilege mappings.

- **Board and object authorization**: Evaluate guild membership, board visibility, mode, restrictions and object ownership on every mutation; isolate tenants and distinguish system/admin actions from ordinary writer operations.

### Writing grants

- **Request to Draw and Grant the Pen**: Implement request queue, moderation approval/denial and targeted writing grants; authorize grant issuers and recipients; reject self-approval and cross-board requests; communicate outcomes.

- **Temporary grant expiry and immediate revocation**: Use server time for TTL; revoke on expiry, moderator action or role removal; fence in-flight writes and active sockets with policy generation; ensure stale clients cannot continue editing.

### Emergency controls

- **Board locking and emergency lockdown**: Persist lock state, authorize unlock, broadcast policy changes and reject concurrent unauthorized writes; ensure emergency lockdown overrides temporary grants and all ordinary writers.

- **User restrictions and moderation panel**: Allow authorized moderators to restrict users, review requests, grant/revoke writing and inspect active permissions; separate UI visibility from backend enforcement; prevent moderator escalation.

### Audit and isolation

- **Moderation audit records**: Record actor, guild, board, policy generation, reason, action and outcome using append-only semantics; avoid tokens and unnecessary content; scope audit access and retention.

- **Authorization adversarial acceptance**: Exercise all allow/deny pairs, wrong guild/board/object, stale roles, expired grants, concurrent revocation, forged administrator flags and replayed actions; require negative tests for every critical rule.
