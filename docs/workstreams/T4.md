# T4: Real-Time Collaboration & Presence

Owner expertise: Realtime Systems. No individual assignment implied.

## Research question

Choose element conflict and operation sequencing strategy; compare authoritative operation log approaches against Excalidraw semantics without full-document last-write-wins.

## Required contract

Specify versioned operation envelopes, per-board revision, idempotency keys, compound atomicity, conflict rules and reconnect protocol.

## Capability scope

### WebSocket connectivity

- **Authenticated board room handshake**: Authenticate WSS upgrades and bind sockets to verified user/guild/board; recheck membership and edit permissions; enforce origin/proxy constraints and reject expired or cross-tenant credentials.

- **Reconnect, late join and snapshot recovery**: Resume from last durably accepted revision; replay missing operations or bounded snapshot plus tail; handle session renewal, duplicate delivery and interrupted transfer without divergence.

### Mutation processing

- **Validated sequencing and idempotency**: Validate versioned operation schema and payload sizes; assign monotonic board revisions; deduplicate by scoped operation ID and return prior result; acknowledge only after durable commit.

- **Concurrent conflict and atomic operations**: Use server-authoritative per-element conflict policy, explicit base revisions and atomic compound operations; reject or transform stale intent deterministically; avoid naive full-document last-write-wins.

### Presence and awareness

- **Participants, live cursors and selections**: Publish bounded ephemeral cursor/selection updates for authorized room members; expire disconnected presence; avoid persistence of cursor history and hidden board participants.

- **Backpressure and WebSocket abuse limits**: Apply per-user and board message/byte budgets; coalesce lossy presence, preserve accepted operations, bound queues and disconnect slow consumers with resumable recovery.

### Convergence and reliability

- **Duplicate, reorder and fault handling**: Specify protocol errors, unsupported versions and replay handling; inject dropped/reordered frames and process crashes; ensure accepted mutations survive restart exactly once at logical level.

- **State convergence verification**: Build deterministic multi-client harness for simultaneous same-object edits, groups, deletion, reconnect and snapshot boundaries; compare canonical board hashes and durable revision history.

## Proposed research decision

[Conflict and sequencing research](../research/collaboration-semantics.md) proposes an
actor/tenant-scoped authoritative log with per-element preconditions and atomic
compounds. Its finite scalar model passes 16 local tests, including 720 positional
replay permutations and all 24 orders of four revisions, on 2026-10-25 using
Python 3.14.3 on Windows. A full buffer accepts the next contiguous revision and
drains successors; a fourth out-of-order insertion still requires recovery.
These are not product, database or platform
results. See the record for exact commands, source versions and unresolved adapter,
codec, transaction, snapshot and acceptance work. #132/#133 and formal review,
merge and post-merge gates remain open.

The toy clears receipts on restore and rejects old lineages before receipt lookup.
It does not verify #481's proposed retired-lineage receipt retention and replay
precedence; the research record documents that explicit compatibility limitation.
